


Algorithm  |  Data Structure Used  |  Primary Use               |  Complexity                          
-----------+-----------------------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------
BFS        |  Queue                |  Shortest path, explore    |  O(V+E)O(V+E)O(V+E)                  
DFS        |  Stack/Recursion      |  Connectivity, cycles      |  O(V+E)O(V+E)O(V+E)                  
Dijkstra   |  Priority Queue       |  Shortest path (weighted)  |  O(E+Vlog⁡V)O(E + V \log V)O(E+VlogV)
A* Search  |  Priority Queue       |  Heuristic shortest path   |  Depends on heuristic
