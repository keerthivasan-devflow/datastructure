
Here's a comprehensive list of key **graph algorithms**, grouped by category to help you navigate them easily:

---

### 🔍 **Traversal Algorithms**
- **Depth-First Search (DFS)**
- **Breadth-First Search (BFS)**

---

### 🛣️ **Shortest Path Algorithms**
- **Dijkstra’s Algorithm** – for weighted graphs with non-negative edges
- **Bellman-Ford Algorithm** – handles negative weights
- **Floyd-Warshall Algorithm** – all-pairs shortest paths
- **A* Search Algorithm** – heuristic-based path finding
- **Johnson’s Algorithm** – all-pairs shortest paths in sparse graphs

---

### 🧩 **Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) Algorithms**
- **Prim’s Algorithm**
- **Kruskal’s Algorithm**

---

### 🔄 **Cycle Detection**
- **DFS-based Cycle Detection**
- **Union-Find (Disjoint Set Union)** – for undirected graphs

---

### 🧠 **Topological Sorting**
- **Kahn’s Algorithm** – BFS-based
- **DFS-based Topological Sort**

---

### 🔗 **Connectivity & Components**
- **Union-Find (Disjoint Set Union)**
- **Tarjan’s Algorithm** – for strongly connected components
- **Kosaraju’s Algorithm** – for strongly connected components
- **Bridge and Articulation Point Detection**

---

### 🧭 **Advanced & Specialized**
- **PageRank Algorithm** – used in search engines
- **Chinese Postman Problem**
- **Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP)** – NP-hard
- **Eulerian Path/Circuit Detection**
- **Hamiltonian Path/Circuit Detection**

---
